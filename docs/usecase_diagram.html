Platform Business Rules

BR-01: Authentication & Security

• Passwords must be hashed using bcrypt (salt rounds ≥ 10) before MongoDB storage.
• Access tokens expire in 15 mins (Redux memory); Refresh tokens expire in 7 days (HttpOnly cookie).
• Mentors start as "pending" and are blocked from login/listing until Admin approves them.

BR-02: IELTS Mock Exams

• IELTS Reading: 3 passages, 40 questions, 60 minutes.
• IELTS Listening: 4 sections, 1 audio file, 40 questions, 30 minutes.
• Graded automatically based on raw score scale from Band 1.0 (0-9 right answers) to Band 9.0 (39-40 right answers).

BR-03: AI Speaking Practice

• System must evaluate & grade voice inputs in < 7 seconds from stopping.
• Local/Cloudinary audio files are wiped immediately upon successful AI processing.
• AI feedback must separately grade Fluency, Lexical, Grammar, and Pronunciation.

BR-04: Booking & Scheduling

• Concurrency is handled with a Redis lock (10s expiration) on slot IDs to prevent double bookings.
• Slots can only start ≥ 1 hour in the future.
• Mentors can edit/delete slots only if they remain unbooked (isBooked = false).

BR-05: Platform Governance

• Unapproved/inactive mentors are hidden from search lists.
• Admins cannot edit their own roles, block themselves, or suspend other administrators.
• Access is strict: Students view only their own records; Mentors edit only notes for slots they hosted.
